## TIER 1: Homepage Flagship Case Studies

These five projects lead the site. They have the strongest proof, the clearest story, and hit the highest-value hiring audiences.

### 1. CTRL+ALT+DEITY.EXE
**Why Tier 1:** Festival selection is hard, verifiable external proof. The end-to-end pipeline story directly demonstrates AI creative production capability — the rarest and most valued skill in the portfolio.
**Hiring audience:** Creative Technologist, AI Creative Producer, AI Content Specialist, Forward Deployed Creative
**Proof available:** Festival selection (Artefact AI Film Festival Paris 2025), full YouTube film, 4 stills
**Proof missing:** Detailed shot count, prompt architecture examples
**Strength:** Exceptional. The only project with a public, verifiable external award.

### 2. Fractal Breaths
**Why Tier 1:** Has a live deployed URL, a YouTube demo video, and clearly demonstrates rapid prototyping and AI-assisted design-to-code. It anchors the entire "Prototype" track. Used as the interactive demo section on the homepage.
**Hiring audience:** Creative Technologist, AI Prototyper, Experience Designer, Forward Deployed Creative
**Proof available:** Live URL (fractal-breaths.netlify.app), YouTube demo video, Notion description
**Proof missing:** Exact build timeline, AI tool attribution in workflow
**Strength:** Strong. Live, deployable, demonstrable.

### 3. GenAI Sessions & AI Bootcamp
**Why Tier 1:** 3 YouTube-documented sessions + a 2-day bootcamp + HIKEathon coaching is strong evidence of sustained AI enablement work. Best project for enablement/education roles.
**Hiring audience:** AI Enablement Lead, AI Trainer, GenAI Educator, Learning Experience Designer
**Proof available:** 3 session videos on YouTube, LinkedIn recaps, HIKEathon coaching image, scale numbers (50+, 85%)
**Proof missing:** Slide decks, promptbook examples, participant photos
**Strength:** Strong. Multiple documented artifacts.

### 4. HIKE Campaign Storytelling
**Why Tier 1:** Two fully documented campaigns with YouTube videos, a real landing page, and clear multi-channel execution. Best project for marketing/content/communications roles.
**Hiring audience:** AI Marketing, Product Marketing, Content Systems, Communications
**Proof available:** 2 YouTube videos, landing page link, full written case study, follower growth numbers
**Proof missing:** Analytics screenshots, social post examples
**Strength:** Strong. Videos and written case study both available.

### 5. GIZ Learning UX & Innovation
**Why Tier 1:** The ~30% engagement improvement and Audience Choice Award are strong institutional proof. Demonstrates work in a different context (public sector / learning systems) that broadens the portfolio.
**Hiring audience:** Learning UX, Digital Transformation, Public Sector Innovation, EdTech
**Proof available:** Written description, results claims
**Proof missing:** Screenshots, GIZ links, DEEGRO.w documentation
**Strength:** Medium. Proof exists but is harder to verify visually right now.

**WHAT I DID**

- **Messaging and story structure:** Defined core narrative that explained program value in simple language
- **Video production:** Concept development, scripting, shooting, editing for both promo and recap content
- **Content packaging:** Created landing page copy, visuals, and social cut-downs optimized for each platform
- **Team collaboration:** Ran feedback loops with HIKE leadership to ensure alignment

**WHAT I DELIVERED**

- **Promo video:** Pre-event teaser to drive sign-ups
- **Recap video:** Post-event highlight reel for ongoing promotion
- **Landing page:** Complete content and visual structure
- **Social assets:** Instagram and LinkedIn versions optimized for each platform

**THE RESULTS**

- Successfully drove event registrations and community engagement
- Complete campaign package delivered on time with consistent look and messaging across all channels
- Campaign assets continue to be reused for future Demo Day marketing
- Maintained clear narrative across 5 content formats: promo video, recap video, landing page, and social assets for LinkedIn and Instagram

## **WHAT THESE CAMPAIGNS DEMONSTRATE**

**Translating Complexity Into Clarity**
Demo Day campaign made a startup pitch event accessible to three different audiences (startups, investors, community) by focusing on concrete outcomes: what participants gain, what investors see, what the community enables. The funding campaign translated abstract policy value ("why public funding matters") into emotional storytelling using real voices from stakeholders who directly benefited.

**Multi-Channel Campaign Execution**
Demo Day required coordinated delivery across 5 formats (promo video, recap video, landing page, LinkedIn, Instagram), each optimized for its platform while maintaining consistent narrative. The funding campaign focused on a single high-impact video designed for both social virality and direct stakeholder outreach, supported by targeted copy for different distribution channels.

**Creating Reusable Sales Assets**
Demo Day deliverables continue to serve HIKE's marketing needs: landing page structure informs future events, video templates guide new campaigns, messaging frameworks are reused for partner outreach. The funding campaign video became HIKE's core advocacy tool, used in grant applications, stakeholder meetings, and community engagement beyond the original campaign.

**Stakeholder Collaboration Under Pressure**
Demo Day involved coordinating with startup founders, HIKE leadership, and external speakers across a tight timeline for a high-visibility public event. The funding campaign required managing sensitive messaging around public funding uncertainty while maintaining optimistic, forward-looking tone that energized rather than panicked stakeholders.

**Goal-Oriented Campaign Design**
Demo Day was structured around measurable conversion: landing page optimized for sign-ups, promo video designed to drive traffic, recap content built for post-event momentum and future recruitment. The funding campaign prioritized emotional resonance and shareability to maximize reach among policymakers and community supporters within a compressed advocacy timeline.

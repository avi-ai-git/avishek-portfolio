# GenAI Sessions & AI Bootcamp

**Category:** Teach
**Hiring audience:** AI Enablement Lead, AI Trainer, GenAI Educator, Learning Experience Designer, AI Adoption Consultant

---

## One-line thesis
Designed and delivered HIKE's GenAI education program — three topic sessions plus a 2-day bootcamp — built for practical workflows and fast adoption, not tool demos.

---

## Context
HIKE is a startup incubator in Nordhausen. The community includes startup founders, students, and practitioners — a mixed technical audience who needed real GenAI skills, not a general overview. I designed and delivered the full learning program from scratch.

---

## Problem / Challenge
How do you move 50+ people from "I've heard of ChatGPT" to building repeatable AI workflows — without overwhelming them? The challenge was practical adoption, not awareness. Every session had to end with something participants could actually use the next day.

---

## My Role
- Session design — agenda, demos, takeaways per topic
- Live facilitation and Q&A
- Prompt and workflow design for each topic
- Packaging learnings into repeatable formats
- HIKEathon GenAI + Design Thinking coaching (annual 2-day event)

---

## Process
1. Program design — mapped three distinct topic areas based on community needs
2. Session 1: NotebookLM — AI-powered research and knowledge management
3. Session 2: Vibe Coding — building MVPs from prompts to product
4. Session 3: AI Music Production — generative audio tools for creators
5. 2-day GenAI Bootcamp — deeper practice, guided projects, certificates
6. Supporting materials — prompt libraries, recap content, Notion knowledge hub
7. HIKEathon coaching — supported teams through GenAI + Design Thinking sprints

---

## Tools / Workflow
- **Research / knowledge:** NotebookLM
- **Vibe coding:** Cursor, Replit, Gemini Canvas
- **AI music:** Suno AI, MusicFX
- **Image / visual AI:** Midjourney
- **Video AI:** Runway, ElevenLabs
- **LLMs:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini
- **Workshop delivery:** Notion knowledge hub, Canva slides, prompt libraries

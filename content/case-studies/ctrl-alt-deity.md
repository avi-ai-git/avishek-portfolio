**Category:** Film / Visual AI
**Hiring audience:** Creative Technologist, AI Creative Producer, Generative AI Producer, Forward Deployed Creative, AI Content Specialist

---

## One-line thesis
An AI short film produced end-to-end — from script to sound — selected for the Artefact AI Film Festival Paris 2025.

---

## Context
I wanted to tell a human story about grief, set in a near-future world where AI is ambient and inevitable. I treated GenAI as a production partner — not a gimmick, not a shortcut. Every visual, every voice, every sound was planned as part of a coherent creative vision before generation began.

---

## Problem / Challenge
How do you produce a visually consistent, emotionally coherent short film entirely through AI — when every image generation run produces something slightly different? The challenge was not "can AI make pretty images" — it was "can 100+ AI-generated assets from different tools, on different days, look and feel like one film?"

---

## My Role
- Writing and direction
- Visual development and prompt design
- AI image generation (Midjourney)
- AI video / motion synthesis (Runway)
- Voice production (ElevenLabs)
- Editing and pacing
- Sound design and final mix

---

## Process
1. Script — original sci-fi narrative focused on grief and near-future AI
2. Style tests — developed visual language and tone before production
3. Character and world consistency system — prompt architecture to maintain design across shots
4. Shot generation — image and video generation per scene using Midjourney + Runway
5. Assembly edit — timeline construction, pacing, scene sequencing
6. Sound design — voice (ElevenLabs) + spatial audio + ambient sound
7. Final mix and export

---

## Tools / Workflow
- **Image generation:** Midjourney
- **Motion / video synthesis:** Runway
- **Voice production:** ElevenLabs
- **Sound design:** Spatial audio tools
- **Editing:** DaVinci Resolve / CapCut
- **Workflow logic:** ComfyUI-style prompt consistency thinking

---

## Output
A coherent AI short film: original story, consistent visual world, original voice performance, and a mixed soundtrack. Viewable in full on YouTube and at the Artefact AI Film Festival gallery.

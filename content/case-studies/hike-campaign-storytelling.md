---

## Campaign 1: #hikemussbleiben — Public Funding Advocacy

### Problem / Challenge
HIKE faced uncertainty around continued public funding after December 2025. The challenge: make a compelling case to government stakeholders and community supporters in under 5 minutes — explaining what a startup incubator actually enables — without sounding panicked.

### What I Did
- Developed script and narrative arc — made the case clear and emotional using real stakeholder voices
- Video production — editing, pacing, sound design, final polish
- Managed multi-speaker interview footage and edited into a cohesive narrative
- Delivered final assets for social distribution and direct advocacy outreach

### Deliverables
- Funding campaign appeal video featuring multiple stakeholders
- Supporting copy and social captions for multi-channel promotion

### Results
- Successfully communicated HIKE's value to public funding stakeholders
- Video became HIKE's core advocacy tool for stakeholder meetings, grant applications, and community engagement

## Campaign 2: HIKE Demo Day 2025 — Event Marketing

### Problem / Challenge
HIKE needed a unified campaign to drive attendance for Demo Day 2025, its flagship startup pitch event. The challenge: consistent messaging across landing page, video, and social that would resonate with three different audiences — startups, investors, and community supporters — without losing clarity.

### What I Did
- Defined core narrative — explained program value in simple, audience-specific language
- Video production — concept development, scripting, shooting, editing (promo and recap)
- Created landing page copy, visuals, and social cut-downs optimized per platform
- Ran feedback loops with HIKE leadership to maintain alignment

### Deliverables
- Pre-event promo video (drive sign-ups)
- Post-event recap video (ongoing promotion)
- Complete landing page content and visual structure
- Social assets for LinkedIn and Instagram

### Results
- Successfully drove event registrations and community engagement
- Campaign assets reused for future Demo Day marketing
- Consistent narrative across 5 content formats: promo video, recap video, landing page, LinkedIn, Instagram
